# 📱 Offline QR Manager - User Manual (用户手册)

Welcome to the Offline QR Manager! This app allows you to scan factory tickets even when the internet is completely disconnected. 

---

## 1. How to Open the App (如何打开应用)

Because this app runs directly on a secure internal IP address, you must follow these specific steps to open it:

1. Open **Chrome** or **Safari** on your phone.
2. Type this exact address into the top search bar:
   👉 **`https://141.147.165.228:7861/offline`**
3. **Important:** You will see a scary red warning that says "Your connection is not private" (您的连接不是私密连接). **This is normal!**
   * **In English:** Click **"Advanced"** at the bottom, then click **"Proceed to 141.147.165.228 (unsafe)"**.
   * **In Chinese:** 点击底部的 **“高级” (Advanced)**，然后点击 **“继续访问 141.147.165.228（不安全）”**.
4. When the app asks for Camera Permissions, you **must** click **Allow (允许)**.

> [!TIP]
> **Bookmark it!** Once the app loads, tap the Share icon and select "Add to Home Screen" (添加到主屏幕) so you can open it like a normal app next time!

---

## 2. Setting Up for the Day (下载今日菲票)

Before you go to the offline warehouse, you need to download the ticket data while you still have Wi-Fi:

1. Open the app.
2. Click the purple **Download Tickets (下载菲票)** button.
3. *Note: The time range is permanently locked to the **Last 3 Months (最近3个月)** to ensure you always have the most relevant data.*
4. A green message will confirm how many tickets were loaded. You are now ready to go offline!

---

## 3. Scanning Tickets Offline (离线扫码)

Once you are in the warehouse (even with no internet or signal):

1. Click the large blue **+ New Box (+ 开始新箱子)** button.
2. **Select your ticket details:** Choose the Customer, Style No, Cut No, and Bundle No. 
3. **Enter Quantity:** Tap the Quantity field to open your number keypad and type the amount.
4. Click **Open Camera (打开相机)** and point your phone at the QR code.
5. Click **Save (保存)**. The ticket is now saved inside this Box!
6. Keep scanning items until the box is full. You can go back to the main screen to start a second box at any time.

---

## 4. Uploading to the Cloud (上传至云端)

At the end of your shift, when you walk back into an area with Wi-Fi or 4G/5G signal:

1. Open the app (ensure your phone says you are connected to the internet).
2. Scroll to the bottom and click the blue **Push to Cloud Database (推送至云端数据库)** button.
3. Wait a few seconds for the green success message. All your scanned boxes are now safely sent to the main company database!

> [!WARNING]
> **Phone Storage Full?**
> If your phone runs out of memory, upload your tickets to the cloud first. Then, click the dark **Export & Clear Local DB (导出所有数据并清除)** button to wipe the phone's memory and start fresh.
